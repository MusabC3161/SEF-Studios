Kimliksiz toplam sayaçlar nedir?

Oyun, yukarıdaki olayları yalnızca adı olan bir sayaca "+1" eklemek biçiminde gönderir (ör. "11 Ekim'de ilk kez açanlar"). Her artış tek başına gider; içinde reklam kimliği, cihaz kimliği, kullanıcı kimliği, ad, e-posta ya da başka bir tanımlayıcı yoktur ve iki artışı birbirine bağlayacak hiçbir şey yoktur. Bu yüzden sayaçlar "kaç kişi" sorusunu yanıtlar, "kim" sorusunu yanıtlamaz. Gün ve sayaç adı dışında hiçbir bilgi saklanmaz. Yükleme sayaçlarının adında kaba bir cihaz sınıfı bulunur (ör. "PowerVR, 3 GB altı, OpenGL"); her sınıfı çok sayıda farklı telefon paylaşır, cihaz modeli ya da cihaza özgü bir bilgi gönderilmez.

Her bağlantıda sunucu, internetin doğası gereği IP adresinizi görür. Biz bu adresi kaydetmeyiz ve sayaçlarla ilişkilendirmeyiz; ancak hizmeti sunan Google kendi işletme kayıtlarını kendi politikasına göre tutabilir. Bu sayaçlar Ayarlar'dan kapatılamaz; kimliksiz oldukları için tek bir kişiye ait olarak ayrıştırılamaz. Yeni sayım istemiyorsanız oyunu kaldırabilir ya da bize yazarak itiraz edebilirsiniz (8. bölüm).

Yurt dışına aktarım, KVKK madde 9 uyarınca açık rızanıza ve/veya hizmet sağlayıcılarla yapılan standart sözleşmelere dayanır.

6. Saklama süresi

Analiz verileri hizmet sağlayıcıların ayarlarında belirlenen süre boyunca (en fazla 14 ay) saklanır, sonra silinir ya da anonim hale getirilir. Kimliksiz toplam sayaçlar kişisel veri içermediği için, oyun yayında olduğu sürece toplam olarak saklanır. Cihazınızdaki oyun verileri, oyunu kaldırana kadar cihazınızda kalır.

7. Çocuklar

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you play Tanks of War - WW2 (Google Play package com.sefstudios.tanksofwar, "the Game").

2. Data that stays on your device

Your progress (missions, stars, silver, tank upgrades) and settings (sound, language, graphics quality, privacy choices) are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. The same save also keeps the day of your first launch (the day only) and which identity-free counter (section 4) has already been sent; this does not leave your device. Everything is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

3. Your consent and choices

On first launch the Game offers three equal choices: Reject all, Options and Accept all. In Options you decide separately on game analytics and personalised ads / ad measurement; the boxes start unticked. Without your consent no data is sent for these two purposes, and the whole Game remains playable. The one exception is the identity-free totals described in section 4: they run whatever you choose and cannot be linked to you. You can change your choices at any time in Settings > Privacy.

What are identity-free totals?

For the events above the Game only adds "+1" to a named counter (for example "first launches on 11 October"). Each increment is sent on its own; it carries no advertising ID, device ID, user ID, name, email or any other identifier, and nothing links two increments. So the counters answer "how many", never "who". Nothing is stored besides the day and the counter's name. The load counters' names include a coarse device class (e.g. "PowerVR, under 3 GB, OpenGL"); many different phones share each class, and no device model or device-specific detail is sent.

On every connection the server inevitably sees your IP address. We do not record it or tie it to the counters, but Google, which runs the service, may keep its own operational logs under its own policy. These counters cannot be switched off in Settings; because they are identity-free, one person's contribution cannot be singled out. To stop being counted you can uninstall the Game, or write to us to object (section 8).

Data may be transferred to the servers of the following providers (mainly in the USA and the EU); their own privacy policies apply:

  • Unity Technologies (game engine): anonymous device and performance statistics. Policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config). Policy
  • Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase): only the identity-free totals (section 4); servers in Europe (eur3). Policy
  • GameAnalytics: game analytics. Policy
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook SDK): ad campaign measurement, only with your consent. Policy
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads): showing ads; non-personalised without your ads consent. Policy
  • Google Play (Install Referrer): where the app was installed from. Policy

Some of these services are added in later versions of the Game; this list is kept up to date before a service goes live.

6. Retention

Analytics data is kept for the period set with each provider (at most 14 months) and then deleted or anonymised. The identity-free totals contain no personal data and are kept as totals for as long as the Game is live. Game data on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its processing, or withdraw your consent at any time (Settings > Privacy). Write to the email above; mentioning your device's advertising ID helps us find your data. We answer free of charge within 30 days. The identity-free totals (section 4) are not tied to a person, so one person's contribution cannot be found or deleted; you may still object to them.

When this policy changes, the date above is updated; significant changes are also announced in the Game.
