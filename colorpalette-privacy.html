Color Palette Generator – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 03.11.2026

This policy explains how the Android app Color Palette Generator (package com.sefstudios.colorpalette, "the App") handles information.

1. Who is responsible

Musab Çelikbaş (SEF Studios) – contact: musabc3161@gmail.com

2. What stays on your device

Your saved palettes, their names, history and settings (theme, language) are stored only on your device. Photos you pick and camera frames are processed on the device to find colors; they are not stored, uploaded or shared. There are no accounts. When you uninstall the App, this data is deleted. A backup file you create yourself is only shared where you choose to send it.

3. Camera and photos

The camera permission is used only for the live color picker, and only while that screen is open. The gallery picker gives the App access only to the image you choose.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Your rights and contact

Because the App keeps your data on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage. For questions or requests about data handled by ad or billing providers, write to musabc3161@gmail.com. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
